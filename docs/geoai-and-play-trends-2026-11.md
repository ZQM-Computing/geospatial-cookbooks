**Research snapshot: 2026-11-06.** Owner: **ZQM Computing** for technical
experiments. The distinct **ZQM Labs** organization owns all player-facing
games, quests, event operation and community moderation. This document is
a sourced research and routing register, not a deployed feature or evidence
of virality. Traffic, conversion and social-view claims require dated,
independently checked platform metrics.

## Culture and games — research, not implementation claims

[GeoHunt](https://geohunt.gg/) publicly advertises browser-based shared
Street View games with room codes, object identification, player voting
and account-free joining. This validates the existence of the format,
**not** its audience size or demonstrated conversion performance.
The existing [ZQM Labs game backlog](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/zqmlabs-site/issues/87)
is the owner for assessing safe alternatives:

- **Invite-only, low-friction challenges:** start with a shareable challenge
  identifier and an original no-login puzzle. Real-time multiplayer,
  leaderboards and durable scores require independent identity/abuse review.
- **Observation bingo:** make original fictional/synthetic scenes or
  licensed illustrations; do not scrape or re-host Google Street View tiles.
  Require content review, accessibility alternatives and permissions.
- **Photo-map recap:** any future EXIF workflow must be opt-in and locally
  processed by default, with coordinates stripped/aggregated before sharing.
  Do not upload private camera rolls or display home/work paths.
- **Location game cousins:** use Munzee/Adventure Lab as conceptual market
  comparators for QR and authored routes, not a claim of IP or feature parity.
- **Livestream spectacle:** no real-time public coordinate drops, dangerous
  physical races, trespassing incentives, or unlicensed cash/prize
  promotions. Propose fictional or scheduled non-location clues instead.
- **Territory play:** fair progression and no pay-to-win mechanisms are
  hypotheses requiring player research; persistent real-world territory
  tracking introduces privacy, safety and abuse requirements.

Named examples such as specific celebrity prize totals, "Mapped"
apps' feature sets, and MapRaiders monetization were not independently
validated in this snapshot. They must not appear as facts in public
marketing without a verifiable, dated primary source.

## Evidence and publication gates

1. **Label the source type:** official product page, controlled benchmark,
   firsthand user research, or unverified social anecdote.
2. **Define the claim and denominator:** exact model/version, task count,
   dates, data license, time/memory/size measurements, or social metrics.
3. **Separate research from launch:** a linked GitHub PR is neither an
   advertised product nor evidence of deployment, purchase availability,
   professional certification or revenue.
4. **Prove reproducibility:** use deterministic seed, environment versions,
   paired outputs and regression tests. Normalize CRS/units before spatial
   comparisons; source-data gaps are "unknown", not zero.
5. **Protect privacy and safety:** don't republish private photo positions,
   expose individuals' location histories, or promote live prize-hunt
   coordinates. For minors, use age-appropriate, consent-based procedures.
6. **Review brand routing:** Computing delivers technical evidence and
   services; Labs owns game mechanics and experiences. A link between
   reviewed artifacts does not establish a shared production dependency.

## First measured experiments

- Computing: six-scenario synthetic spatial-AI evaluation, with explicit
  pass/fail and unanswered states, no remote model call.
- Computing: exact local file bytes and timings from the synthetic
  GeoParquet/GeoJSON comparison, without unverified Shapefile ratios.
- Labs: an opt-in shareable challenge score from the isolated Daily Geo
  Mystery prototype, then research an original illustrated bingo and
  privacy-safe friend-duel experience.

Track qualified professional inquiries separately from GitHub visits, and
game return sessions separately from copied share text. None of these
metrics has an observed ZQM baseline in this document.
